### Projects
My projects make use of a recently developed path-integral based approach to solve for the dynamics in open quantum systems, Trotter's decomposition with linked cluster expansion [[1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.10977)]. This approach gives us an *exact* solution to various system dynamics in the presence of phonons. I developed a modified algorithm which addressed a major limitation - only systems that have a cavity channel (or any mode uncoupled to phonons) were solvable. Now we are able to treat qubit only systems, exploring various coupling mechanisms, such as Förster coupling. The result of this research can have various applications, from biology to quantum computing. 

As a result of this development, we found a novel and counterintuitive mechanism for reducing, including entirely suppressing, the decoherence of coupled qubits by utilising the coherence of the the phonon bath [[2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.14685)]. 

Due to the large tensor networks required to achieve convergence in some systems, which become very computationally expensive, we have developed and implemented a conceptually simple optimisation scheme. To highlight the extent of the optimisation scheme, the newly achievable calculations, which are run on a standard office computer with 16GB RAM would require over 50,000,000 GB of RAM using the old method! Furthermore, calculations which would take many days now take a matter of seconds. 

[The repository of the optimisation scheme to solve for the dynamics of all these systems can be found here](https://github.com/HallL415/Optimisation)
